Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 27, 2026 | Last Updated: March 27, 2026

Value to Victory ("we," "our," "us") operates valuetovictory.com, assessment.valuetovictory.com, and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number (optional) when you create an account, take an assessment, or subscribe.
  • Assessment Responses: Your answers to assessment questions, which generate your Pillar Strength Map and growth area recommendations.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card details on our servers.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time on site, and interaction patterns to improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your personalized assessment results and Pillar Strength Maps
  • To deliver your membership benefits and content
  • To send your Value Engine prescription, challenge prompts, and program updates
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To improve our assessment tools and user experience

Couple Plan Transparency Disclosure

If you enroll in a Couple plan (Value Builder or Victory VIP), all assessment results are shared between both partners by design. This includes individual scores, Pillar Strength Maps, growth area recommendations, and challenge progress. Both partners consent to this shared visibility upon enrollment. This transparency is the foundation of the program.

Children's Privacy

Children Under 13: We do not knowingly collect, use, or store any personal data from children under 13 years of age. If you are under 13, you may not use this program.

Children 13–17: Minors between 13 and 17 may participate only under a parent or guardian's account. No separate account or data collection occurs for minors.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

  • Stripe — Payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • HubSpot — Email delivery and CRM (hubspot.com/privacy)
  • Vercel — Website hosting

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption, secure hosting, and access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights (CCPA — California Residents)

If you reside in California, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

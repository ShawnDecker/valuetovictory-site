What this is

A book about what things are actually worth — and why almost nobody can tell you.

I have spent my working life putting a number on other people's property. Twelve thousand times. Long enough to notice that the number was never really the point.

Value is not a price. Price is what a thing costs. Value is what it returns to the person holding it — and that changes depending on who is holding it, what they know, who they know, and how much time they have left to use it.

That gap is where people lose money, lose years, and lose the things they cannot buy back. This book is about closing it.

The framework

Time for P.I.N.K. — the four things every valuation actually runs on.

Four inputs, measured inside the one resource you cannot make more of. Strip any valuation down far enough and this is what is underneath it — in a house, in a business, and in a life. Here is how each one pays you back.

P

People

Who is around you, and what that access is worth.

  • See your circle clearly — who adds to your life and who quietly drains it.
  • Invest in the right relationships instead of spreading yourself thin across all of them.
  • Stop carrying people who will not walk with you, without guilt or drama.
  • Build the kind of trust that opens doors money cannot.
I

Influence

What moves when you speak, and how far it carries.

  • Be heard the first time — at home, at work, and in the room that matters.
  • Earn influence through value you deliver, not volume or title.
  • Lead without pushing, so people follow because they want to.
  • Make your word count — a reputation that walks in ahead of you.
N

Numbers

What the arithmetic says when nobody is flattering you.

  • Know what things are actually worth, not what someone is asking for them.
  • Stop leaving money on the table in deals, raises, and everyday decisions.
  • Read the numbers honestly, so they serve you instead of scare you.
  • Make big decisions with confidence instead of hope.
K

Knowledge

What you know that cannot be taken off you.

  • Turn what you know into what you are worth — experience is an asset.
  • Learn what actually moves your life, and skip what only fills time.
  • Spot the blind spots that keep costing you before they cost you again.
  • Pass it on — knowledge that outlives you is value that compounds.
T

within Time

The one input that only ever spends down.

  • See where your hours really go — and get some of them back.
  • Spend time on what returns value, not just what feels urgent.
  • Protect the hours that belong to your family, your faith, and your health.
  • Live like time is the asset it is — because it is the only one you cannot earn back.
